# Contributing

## House rules
- **Voice:** a calm manual. Write plainly and steadily, and don't lean on jokes.
- **Chapter shape:** every chapter has four parts: Do This Now (one page), The Craft, Thrive Hacks, and a Checklist.
- **Sources:** every safety-critical number (dosing, rehydration, first aid) carries a `[src: …]` tag that points to WHO, CDC, the Red Cross or an equivalent. A number without a source will fail lint.
- **Out of scope:** weapons, violence and tactical content, and medicine beyond first aid and home care.
- **Region:** not region-specific. Put climate variants (hot, wet, cold) in side-notes.

## Found an error?
Open an issue with the chapter, the claim, and a better source. Errors in safety numbers are fixed first.
