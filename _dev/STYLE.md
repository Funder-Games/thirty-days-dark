# Chapter style spec (every chapter)
File: manuscript/NN-slug.md. Target ~4,000 words (lint band 3,000–5,500). Plain calm manual voice; UK/intl English; no jokes carrying weight; no weapons/violence/tactics; medicine = first aid and home care only; no personal details.

Structure, in order:
# Chapter N · Title
> one-line promise
## Do This Now          (≤ 250 words; must fit one A5 page)
## The Craft            (the how-to; ### subsections; quick-glance tables)
## Thrive Hacks         (creative ideas)
## Checklist            (- [ ] items)

Boxes (blockquote with bold label on first line):
> **Village Hack** — …
> **Day-X Challenge** — …
> **What I Wish I'd Stocked** — …
> **Climate note (hot|wet|cold)** — …
Image placeholder: `[IMAGE: one-line art brief]` on its own line.

Sources: every safety-critical number (doses, concentrations, times, temperatures, volumes, ratios for water, food safety, first aid, rehydration, CO, electrical) ends with `[src: ORG — document title, year]`. Orgs: WHO, CDC, Red Cross/IFRC, UNICEF, NHS, FEMA, EPA, or equivalent national public-health body. Only cite what you are confident the source says; if unsure, soften to non-numeric guidance rather than invent. No TODO/TBD/XXX.
